High Explosive is a personal BattleTech site. It exists to put a few things
somewhere permanent: tools that were useful enough to be worth sharing, house
rules that survived a campaign, and the games and designs that came out of
both.

There is no schedule and no plan for what gets published. Things appear when
they are finished.

## What you can do with any of it

Original material here — rules text, unit writeups, the tools — is free to
use, adapt and pass on at your own table. Attribution is welcome and not
required. If you find something wrong, it is worth telling me about; errors
get corrected in place rather than quietly removed.
